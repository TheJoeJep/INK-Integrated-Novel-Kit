# /synopsis — the full synopsis

> **Tier 4 — generative.** Raw material to react against, not a finished document. But this
> genre has one hard formal rule the other Tier 4 skills don't: **nothing is withheld.** A
> synopsis that holds back the ending isn't a weaker synopsis — it's not doing the job.

**Say this on invocation:** *Drafting a full synopsis — present tense, ending included, no
suspense held back. That's not optional; agents and editors read a synopsis specifically to check
whether the ending works.*

**Source:** [`craft/publishing/publishing.md`](../../craft/publishing/publishing.md)

---

## The rule that makes a synopsis a synopsis

**Present tense.** **The whole plot, including the ending.** No cliffhangers, no "you'll have to
read the book to find out." A query's hook is allowed to create intrigue; a synopsis exists
precisely so an agent or editor can verify the ending pays off the setup without reading the full
manuscript first. Withholding it defeats the document's purpose.

If a synopsis draft leaves the ending vague, that's not restraint — it's a failure to write a
synopsis.

---

## Causal chain, not a list of events

**The single most common way a synopsis goes wrong: it becomes "and then this happens, and then
this happens."** A synopsis is not a chronology. It has to show that each beat happens **because**
of what came before it — conflict and struggle make a story; a sequence of facts doesn't, no
matter how accurately it's reported.

**The test, beat by beat:** can you replace "and then" with "which forces," "because," or "so"
without the sentence breaking? If not, the causal link is missing, and the beat needs either a
stated reason or reconsideration of whether it belongs.

Weak: *He finds the dragon in the shed. Then he investigates the sighting downtown. Then he
learns about the cult's records.*

Working: *He finds the wounded dragon in his own shed — which means the sighting downtown wasn't
a stranger's problem, it's arrived at his door — so he goes looking for anyone who's dealt with
this before, which is how he finds the cult's records.*

## Procedure

**1. Confirm scope.** Whole-manuscript synopsis by default. If an editor or agent has requested a
partial (a one-page version, or a synopsis of only the first act alongside partial pages), confirm
that before drafting — the compression target changes what survives the cut.

**2. Build the beat list.** Pull from `plot/` and `manuscript/_status.md`, in act order. Don't
invent a beat that isn't in the plot documents or the manuscript — **if the ending isn't decided
yet, say that plainly and stop rather than filling it in.** A synopsis of an undecided ending
isn't raw material, it's a guess wearing the document's clothes.

**3. State the causal link for every beat**, not just the beat itself. If you can't state why a
beat follows from what precedes it, flag it rather than smoothing over the gap with "and then."

**4. Trim to what's causally load-bearing.** A subplot or secondary character only earns space if
its resolution is part of *why* the ending happens the way it does. Compress or cut what's
texture rather than causation.

**5. Draft in present tense**, ending stated outright.

---

## Length

Requested synopsis length varies by agent and editor — a one-page version and a full
chapter-by-chapter version are both common asks, and which one's wanted isn't something to assume.
Confirm the target length, or draft a tight version and note that a fuller one is available if
needed.

## Output format

```
── SYNOPSIS DRAFT ── whole manuscript, option A (tight)

  [Present-tense prose, full plot through the ending, causal language
  throughout — "which forces," "because," "so" doing the connective work.]

── SYNOPSIS DRAFT ── option B (fuller)

  [A longer pass with more of the mid-book causal chain spelled out, for
  a request that wants more than a page.]

── CAUSAL CHECK ──
  ⚠ ch14→ch15: currently reads "and then he confronts the cult" — no stated
    reason he chooses confrontation over hiding. Flag for the author.
  ✓ all other transitions carry a because/so/which-forces link.

── ENDING ──
  Stated in full above. Not held back.
```

---

## Rules

- **Present tense, always.**
- **The ending is always included, stated plainly.** Never a version that trails off or teases.
- **Every beat needs a causal link to what precedes it.** Where the link is missing, flag it —
  don't paper over it with "and then" and move on.
- **Don't invent a beat, character, or ending not already established** in `plot/` or the
  manuscript. If the ending isn't decided, say so and stop.
- **Offer at least two lengths or framings**, not one locked draft — the requested length varies
  by recipient.
- **Cut what isn't causally load-bearing**, but say what you cut and why, so the author can
  disagree.
- **This produces a draft document, not canon.** Don't write it into `canon/` or `story/` unless
  the author asks it saved.
